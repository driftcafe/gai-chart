# Hila - Elevator Pitch

## The One-Liner

**AI-powered chart generation from natural language—without ever exposing your sensitive data.**

---

## The Problem

Traditional BI tools (Tableau, Power BI) require:
- Hours of training and configuration
- $50-500/month per user
- Complex interfaces

AI tools (ChatGPT) require:
- Sending your sensitive data to external APIs
- Privacy and compliance risks

---

## The Solution

**Hila** lets you create charts by just asking:

```
"Show me revenue vs costs"
"Add margin"
"Make it a bar chart"
```

**The innovation:** AI only sees column names, never your actual data.

---

## The Demo

**4 queries, 10 seconds, less than a penny:**

1. "Show me revenue vs costs" → Line chart appears
2. "Add margin to see profitability" → Adds third line
3. "Make it a bar chart" → Switches visualization
4. "Which quarter had the best margins?" → Shows all quarters

**Each query builds on the last. No starting over. Just conversation.**
